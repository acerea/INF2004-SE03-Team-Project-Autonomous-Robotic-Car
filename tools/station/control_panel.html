DRIVE

Calibrate while stopped, with the middle sensor over the line and room to turn. The car turns left, right and back on the spot (a few seconds) so every sensor sees black and white. In FAULT it only calibrates standing still.

CRUISE SPEED

30 mm/s requested
car reports: --

10-150 mm/s in 10 mm/s steps (sent to the car as cm/s in a MODE command). The car stops for anything within 15 cm; there is no slow-down band any more.

NUDGE

cm

Straight, 1-63 cm, at the CRUISE SPEED above (APPLY it first to change). Only while the car is IDLE or STOPPED (press STOP first). Reverse has no line steering.

BARCODE ACTIONS

Letters not listed use the car's built-in table (A left, B right, C straight, D U-turn). station.py keeps this list and sends it again whenever the car restarts.
